# shlack!

**A top-down action roguelite where a cartoonist is trapped inside his own satirical magazine.**

## About

On a rainy Sunday, cartoonist Muhittin sneaks into his magazine office, passes out, and wakes up inside the latest issue. To escape, he has to fight the very characters he drew. Every loss restarts the run, and every loop makes him stronger.

shlack! is a **bullet-heaven roguelite** in a Turkish caricature art style. Bosses, weapons, items and playable characters all come from Turkish and Balkan folk and caricature culture.

▶️ **[Watch the trailer on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5127870/shlack/)**

## Features

- Many weapons and items, auto-firing bullet-heaven combat
- Short, fast runs
- Unique boss fights
- Junkman in-game market and meta progression
- Gambling with cartoon money
- Dodging the food courier while catching food

## Development

- **Engine:** Unity (C#)
- **Team:** 4 developers, started as a graduation project at İstanbul Bilgi University
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS, Linux (Steam), Mobile
- **Localization:** 7 languages (EN, TR, FR, IT, DE, ES, ES-LATAM)
- **Playtesting:** 100+ external playtesters
